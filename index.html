Choosing a method. For most business property acquired after Jan 19, 2025, 100% bonus depreciation applies automatically unless you opt out, and it can create a loss. Section 179 is an election limited to your business income. Spreading the cost over years means opting out of both, which people sometimes do when income is low this year but expected to be higher later. Assets placed in service before Jan 20, 2025 had different bonus rates. Spread-over-years estimates use the standard half-year table (5-year: 20%, 32%, 19.2%, 11.52%, 11.52%, 5.76%). If more than 40% of a year’s purchases fall in the last quarter, a different convention applies. Sales: gain up to the depreciation taken is generally ordinary income reported on Form 4797, and a loss is generally deductible. This is a record-keeping aid, not tax advice.
